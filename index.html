This website is for people to help communicate using different types of speech. I want to connect people no matter their disability, language, or lifestyle.

About Me

Hi, I'm Kalel Page-Allan, a employee at The Center. As a new Dsp (Direct Support Porfessional) and AMAP (Approved Medication Assistive Personnel). Ive been working here for a few months now and i have notice a concern or issue with the communication for the Staff and Guys/Individuals who we take care of. Sometimes we cant understand what they are asking for or talking about. I think that is a huge problem considering that us staff who work at the resident and are with them for 8 hours and we wont be able to know what they want. So i wanna make this website and app to help our Guys/Individuals better communicate what they want easily.
